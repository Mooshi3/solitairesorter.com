Tidy your desktop into decks.

SolitaireSorter files desktop icons under other icons. Click a deck's + box and its cards deal out across the desktop like solitaire. Click it again and they gather back up.

What it does

A♠

Make any icon a deck

Right-click any desktop icon and make it a deck. Drop things on its + box to file them under it. The icon itself still opens like before.

2♥

Deals out like cards

Pick how the cards come out, from a fan to a cascade, and how fast and bouncy they are. They land on free spots and never cover your other icons.

3♣

Decks inside decks

A card can be a deck too, with its own + box. Open one inside another and they reopen together next time.

4♦

Make it yours

Box colors, your own box pictures and animated GIFs, card sizes, and a free layout that sizes cards by how much you use them.

5♠

The whole desktop as cards

If you like, every desktop icon becomes a card, with Explorer's own right-click menu, drag and drop, and keys like F2, Delete and Ctrl+C.

6♥

Undo everything

Nothing you file is ever deleted. Undo, or uninstalling, puts every file back where it came from.

Safe on your PC

  • It never goes online. The program itself has no network code at all. (The small setup downloads Microsoft's .NET if your PC needs it, and that's all.)
  • It never needs admin rights. It installs and runs just for you.
  • It's light. Sitting idle it uses about 25 MB of memory and almost no CPU, and it steps aside while a fullscreen game is running.
  • It doesn't change Windows. It draws its cards on top of your desktop, and turning it off leaves everything as it was.

Questions

Where do my files go?

Into a folder called Desktop Decks, next to your Desktop folder. Each deck is an ordinary folder named after its icon, so you can always open it in Explorer.

Will it slow down my games?

No. It does almost nothing while you're not using the desktop, and while a fullscreen game or video is in front it stops watching the mouse completely.

How do I uninstall it?

From Settings > Apps, like any program. It asks whether to put everything from your decks back on the desktop first, or leave your decks as they are.

Why does Windows warn me when I run the setup?

New programs from small developers get that warning until enough people have used them. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
